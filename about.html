Social Butterfly Media

An independent studio making long, calm soundscapes for sleep.

Social Butterfly Media produces original ambient videos — slow, 10-hour rain soundscapes for sleep, rest and focus — and publishes them on channels it owns, starting with Slow Rain Rooms on YouTube.

How our videos are made

Every rain soundscape is synthesised by our own software rather than recorded, so it never repeats. Each video's scene is generated with AI and gently animated, and is disclosed as AI-generated wherever a platform asks. We do not reuse other people's content.

Social Butterfly, the tool

Social Butterfly is the internal software we use to plan, produce and publish our videos. It uses the YouTube API Services only to manage our own channels: setting channel branding, uploading our own videos and setting their details. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
