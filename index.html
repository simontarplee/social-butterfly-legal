Social Butterfly Media — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026.

Social Butterfly Media ("we", "us") operates Social Butterfly, an internal software tool we use to plan, produce and publish our own original videos to YouTube channels that we own and operate. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy explains what the tool accesses, stores and shares.

YouTube API Services

Social Butterfly uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What we access and why

When a channel owner (only us) authorises Social Butterfly for a YouTube channel, the tool accesses, through the YouTube API Services:

  • the authorised channel's identifier, title and handle — to confirm it is the intended channel;
  • the channel's branding settings — to set its title, description, keywords, country, language and banner;
  • video upload and video settings for that channel — to upload our own videos, set their thumbnail, audience (made for kids or not), AI-content disclosure and publish time.

The tool may also check whether a proposed channel handle is already in use. It does not access any other person's account, private data, comments, subscribers or viewing history, and it does not compute or store metrics about other channels.

What we store

  • Authorisation tokens for our own channels, encrypted by the operating system's credential store on the machine that runs the tool. Kept until access is revoked.
  • Identifiers of our own channels and videos, so the tool can manage what it published.
  • Any other data obtained from the YouTube API Services is stored only as long as needed for the task, and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

What we share

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through the YouTube API Services with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube to perform the actions above.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time through Google's security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens; we then delete them. To ask us to delete any data, email the address below and we will do so within 7 days.

Cookies

The tool runs locally and sets no cookies or tracking.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change, with a new effective date. Contact: social.butterfly.media.ai@gmail.com
